Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who uses this app

Secret Seoul Stay Automation is a private Google Apps Script project used only by the host of Secret Seoul Stay with the host's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses and why

  • Gmail: reads booking confirmation, cancellation and inquiry emails from Airbnb in the host's mailbox, and sends reminder emails to the host.
  • Google Sheets: keeps the host's booking sheet up to date.
  • Google Drive and Slides: stores the host's background and artist images and makes a welcome image for each guest.
  • Google Photos: adds the welcome image to an album that the app created, which the host's TV shows as a screensaver.
  • Spotify (with the host's own Spotify account): starts music on the host's speaker before a guest arrives.

Sharing and storage

Data stays in the host's own Google and Spotify accounts. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Guest names and dates are used only to prepare that guest's stay and are moved to an archive tab after checkout.

Removing access

The host can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
