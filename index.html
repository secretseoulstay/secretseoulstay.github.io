Secret Seoul Stay Automation

This is a private, single-user tool used by the host of Secret Seoul Stay, a guesthouse in Seoul, to run their own guest welcome routine.

It reads the host's own booking emails and calendar, keeps a booking sheet up to date, prepares a welcome image for each guest, and starts music on the host's own speaker before check-in.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
